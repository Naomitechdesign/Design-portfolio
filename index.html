WHAT I OFFER

Editing that works with
the way you create.

From full YouTube videos to scroll-stopping social clips, I shape your footage into content that feels clear, polished and easy to watch.

01

Long-form YouTube

Complete edits for vlogs, lifestyle videos and creator-led content with thoughtful pacing and clean storytelling.

  • Story-focused cuts
  • Captions and text
  • Color and audio polish
  • Motion graphics when needed
02

Short-form Videos

Vertical content designed to catch attention quickly and keep the viewer moving through the story.

  • Strong opening hooks
  • Fast, natural pacing
  • On-screen captions
  • Reels, TikToks and Shorts
03

Content Repurposing

Turn one longer piece of content into several useful social clips without losing its original message.

  • Best moments selected
  • Vertical reframing
  • Platform-ready edits
  • Multiple clips from one video
04

Content Scripting

Simple, natural scripts that give your videos structure while still sounding like you.

  • Strong hooks
  • Clear video structure
  • Natural talking points
  • Creator-focused storytelling
